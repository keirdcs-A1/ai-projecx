AI Lab Tycoon.

A playable strategy prototype about dividing an AI lab’s compute between capability, serving and understanding.

PrototypeStrategyAI labsPlayable prototype
Play the prototype
THE QUESTION

Can a three-way resource split create meaningful strategic tension?

The approach

Allocate compute, advance the simulation and observe capability, costs, competition and the gap in understanding. This is the current prototype, not the larger game described in its design proposals.

Using it here

Working title and experimental balance. Values are simulation inputs, not forecasts or investment advice. No real models, financial transactions or external AI services run. Progress lasts for the current page session.
