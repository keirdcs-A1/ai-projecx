Evolution Sandbox.

A playable artificial-evolution sandbox with heritable genomes, evolving neural networks and a world you can observe or influence.

THE QUESTION

What behaviours emerge when creatures inherit their bodies and brains, and survival does the selecting?

  • Seeded worlds and heritable genomes
  • Creature inspection, overlays and player modes
  • Browser-local saves and JSON export

The approach

Run the existing Choose Life browser code: a seeded ecosystem, numeric neural-network brains, metabolism, reproduction and mutation. Observe the world, inspect creatures, change overlays or use the available player modes. No language model or explicit fitness score drives the simulation.

Using it here

Runs locally in your browser and may use substantial CPU. Game snapshots and templates use browser-local IndexedDB; they are not sent to our server. Save imports/exports are local JSON files. It is an experimental simulation with evolving mechanics, not a validated biological model. A larger screen is recommended.
