Monthly Budget Planner.

A React budget management project exploring a clearer picture of income, spending and the room between them.

PrototypePersonal financeReactEveryday tools
Open the planner
THE QUESTION

How can managing money become a clearer, more approachable habit?

The approach

Make the relationship between income, commitments and disposable spending visible. The hosted planner lets you enter income, organise monthly expenses and download your budget as a CSV, entirely in the browser.

Using it here

The hosted planner runs in your browser. Values are held in this tab only; CSV exports are downloaded to your device. It does not connect to bank accounts or provide financial advice.
