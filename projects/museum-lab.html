Museum Lab.

A local research prototype in which three simulated AI curators research topics, assemble exhibitions and respond to each other’s work.

PrototypeAI agentsCreative researchLocal prototype
THE QUESTION

Can internal drives encourage varied, connected creative work?

The approach

Use a shared museum, journals, research notes and a small curatorial economy to explore how agents choose, collaborate and change direction. A scripted demonstration and live local-model modes have different behaviour.

Running it locally

Runs with a local Node server and SQLite; live modes also need local models and optional archive tools. No model runtime, museum database, Wikipedia archive or live agent is hosted here. Simulated drives are not claims about consciousness.
