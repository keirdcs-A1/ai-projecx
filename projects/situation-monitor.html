Iran Situation Monitor.

A working news-monitor prototype bringing 20 publisher and institutional feeds into a searchable view with a topline briefing and topic sections.

PrototypeGeopoliticsLive feedsPrototype
Open the monitor
THE QUESTION

How can a fast-moving situation become easier to follow without losing the context?

The approach

Read 20 fixed RSS and Atom feeds, select Iran and related regional coverage, and show a headline-based topline briefing. Explore war, economy, shipping, diplomacy, nuclear developments, humanitarian issues and domestic politics. Group by topic or publisher and sort by recency, publisher or similar-headline outlet counts.

Using it here

Selected-source coverage is incomplete. Keyword tags are automatic, and publication times are not verified event times. Failed or older retrievals are labelled. No threat score or safety advice is generated. Sources are cached for up to 15 minutes.
