Wikipedia Sprint.

A live Wikipedia link-racing game from the local project originally called Experiment. Find a route from one random article to another before the clock runs out.

PrototypeWikipediaCuriosityPlayable
Play Wikipedia Sprint
THE QUESTION

How quickly can you find a connection between two apparently unrelated subjects?

  • Live random Wikipedia challenges
  • Three-minute countdown and route history
  • No account or saved gameplay tracking

The approach

Load a pair of live Wikipedia articles, start the three-minute timer and move only through article links. Track your route and move count, go back when needed and reroll for a fresh challenge.

Using it here

Live mode contacts Wikipedia directly after you choose to load a challenge; article titles and network information reach Wikipedia. Routes remain in tab memory. External article markup is rebuilt through a tag allowlist. Article attribution and licence links are included.
