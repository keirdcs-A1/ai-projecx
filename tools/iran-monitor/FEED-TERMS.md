# Feed publishers and reuse checks

Checked 22 November 2026. These are practical publication decisions for this exported public, curated headline snapshot. A working RSS address alone does not establish permission to republish it. “Unknown” means permission was not established, rather than a claim that the publisher forbids every use. No publisher has been contacted and no extra licence has been obtained. Ordinary links to publisher home pages remain in the source directory.

Only UK FCDO and The Jerusalem Post headline text and article links are enabled in `reuse-policy.json`. Other feeds are retrieved to check health and count relevant items, but their headline text and article URLs are discarded before the public snapshot is written. No article bodies, descriptions, photographs, logos, audio or video are published. A publisher can change its conditions: review this file and the policy before enabling more sources.
